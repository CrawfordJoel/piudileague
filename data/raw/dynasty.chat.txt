@all

Here are some dynasty league rule items I’d like to bring to the table for a vote. I think these changes would benefit the league’s long-term health, improve roster-building strategy, and make the league feel more like a true dynasty league.

1. Remove QB roster limits

I do not think QB roster limits belong in dynasty, especially in Superflex. Managers should be able to build around QB depth if they want.


2. Remove TE roster limits

TE depth matters in dynasty, and managers should not be capped on how many developmental or backup TEs they can hold.


3. Remove kickers

Kicker feels out of place in dynasty. It adds randomness without much long-term roster strategy.

4. Increase overall roster size

Our current roster size feels small for a normal dynasty league. Dynasty should reward depth, prospect development, and long-term roster construction.

5. Increase taxi squad from 3 to 5 spots

Since this is dynasty, we should prioritize developing young players. A larger taxi squad gives rebuilding teams and patient managers more room to hold prospects.

6. Increase IR spots from 2 to 4 spots

Larger dynasty rosters should have more IR flexibility than normal redraft fantasy. Injuries should not force teams to drop developmental players too aggressively.

7. Vote on TEP scoring

TEP means Tight End Premium. The proposal would make TE receptions worth 1.5 PPR, meaning TEs get a 0.5 point bonus per reception compared to other positions. This would help make TE as important as a normal position (WR/RB) instead of feeling like a weak or throwaway roster spot.

8. Add either a 3rd regular flex or 2nd TE spot

I would like us to vote on adding either a 3rd regular flex or a 2nd TE spot. A 3rd flex would make roster depth matter more, reward better dynasty team-building, and give managers more lineup flexibility with the players they develop. A 2nd TE would also pair well with TEP and help make TE feel like a real position instead of a weak spot.

9. Replace league veto with commissioner veto for collusion only

I would like us to vote on replacing league vetoes with commissioner veto for clear collusion or cheating only. Dynasty trades are long-term moves, and managers should be allowed to value players, picks, timelines, and team direction differently. Trades should not be blocked just because the league thinks one side gained value, or because managers are jealous that another team made a good move.
